Westeros

Westeros is the largest continent in the known world, spanning thousands of miles from the frozen wastes beyond the Wall in the north to the deserts of Dorne in the south. The continent is divided into Nine Regions, often called the Seven Kingdoms after the unification under Targaryen rule.

The Realm is governed by a single Crown, seated in King's Landing at the Crownlands. Below the Crown lie the great Houses, each ruling their own region and answering to the throne.

The North

The North is the largest region by area, a vast expanse of forests, mountains, and harsh winters. The people of the North are hardy, loyal, and bound by strong traditions of honor and duty. The North is ruled from Winterfell, a massive castle built around natural hot springs.

Capital

Winterfell, seat of House Stark

Geography

Forests, mountains, and long winters. The Wall stands at the far north, separating the Realm from the lands beyond.

Major Houses

House Stark (Warden of the North), House Bolton, House Karstark, House Umber, House Manderly, House Mormont

Culture

Ancient traditions, loyalty to the Stark name, and worshipping of the Old Gods mark the Northern character.

The Vale of Arryn

The Vale is a region of mountains and fertile valleys, protected by high peaks that make invasion difficult. The Vale is ruled from the Eyrie, a fortress built high in the mountains, accessible only by narrow passes and winding roads.

Capital

The Eyrie, seat of House Arryn

Geography

High mountains, fertile valleys, and protected passes. The Eyrie sits on a peak nearly unreachable by conventional siege.

Major Houses

House Arryn (Warden of the Vale), House Royce, House Baelish, House Waynwood, House Corbray

Culture

Proud, honor-bound, and protective of their mountain home. The Vale lords value martial skill and ancient custom.

The Riverlands

The Riverlands are a region of fertile river valleys and plains, crisscrossed by major waterways including the Trident. This region is often a battleground due to its position between north and south. The Riverlands are ruled from Riverrun, a castle built at the confluence of three rivers.

Capital

Riverrun, seat of House Tully

Geography

River valleys, fertile plains, and numerous waterways. The Trident is the largest river and defines much of the region.

Major Houses

House Tully (Warden of the Riverlands), House Freys, House Blackwoods, House Brackens, House Vance, House Darry

Culture

The Riverlands are known for hospitality and strength, but suffer greatly in times of war due to their position.

The Crownlands

The Crownlands are the lands immediately surrounding King's Landing and are ruled directly by the Crown. This region includes the capital city, fertile farmlands, and the Blackwater Bay. The Crownlands are the seat of royal power and the heart of the Realm.

Capital

King's Landing, seat of the Iron Throne

Geography

Fertile lands surrounding King's Landing, including farms, villages, and the Blackwater Bay.

Major Houses

House Targaryen (historically), House Baratheon (current), House Velaryon, House Celtigar, House Sunglass

Culture

The center of power, culture, and intrigue. King's Landing is a city of merchants, lords, and those seeking power.

The Reach

The Reach is the most fertile region in Westeros, known for its vast fields of grain and abundance of food. It is a region of great wealth and power. The Reach is ruled from Highgarden, a magnificent castle surrounded by gardens and known for its beauty.

Capital

Highgarden, seat of House Tyrell

Geography

Fertile plains, vast fields of grain, rivers, and abundant natural resources. The Mander is the major river of the region.

Major Houses

House Tyrell (Warden of the Reach), House Hightower, House Florent, House Redwyne, House Tarly, House Rowan

Culture

Known for courtesy, culture, and strength. The Reach lords are educated, wealthy, and politically sophisticated.

The Stormlands

The Stormlands are a region known for its violent storms and rocky terrain. The region is ruled from Storm's End, a massive castle built to withstand the storms. The Stormlands are fierce and proud, with a martial tradition.

Capital

Storm's End, seat of House Baratheon

Geography

Rocky terrain, storm-prone coasts, and the Dornish Marches. The region is defined by its harsh weather.

Major Houses

House Baratheon (Warden of the Stormlands), House Estermont, House Errol, House Buckler, House Swann

Culture

The Stormlands are known for pride, martial prowess, and a fierce independent spirit. Storm's End is legendary for its strength.

Dorne

Dorne is the southernmost region, a land of deserts, mountains, and a fiercely independent people. Dorne was the last region to be conquered by the Targaryens and maintains a unique culture and legal system. Dorne is ruled from Sunspear.

Capital

Sunspear, seat of House Martell

Geography

Deserts, mountains, and the Red Mountains. Dorne is hot, arid, and difficult to invade.

Major Houses

House Martell (Prince of Dorne), House Yronwood, House Dayne, House Uller, House Vaith, House Jordayne

Culture

Dornish culture is unique, with different laws regarding inheritance and a reputation for passion, pride, and independence.

The Westerlands

The Westerlands are a region of mountains and valleys, wealthy due to vast reserves of gold and other metals. The region is ruled from Casterly Rock, a massive fortress built into a mountain. The Westerlands are known for their wealth and military power.

Capital

Casterly Rock, seat of House Lannister

Geography

Mountains, valleys, and rich mineral deposits. Casterly Rock is built into a mountain and is nearly impregnable.

Major Houses

House Lannister (Warden of the Westerlands), House Crakehall, House Lefford, House Prester, House Swyft, House Serrett

Culture

The Westerlands are known for wealth, military power, and sophisticated culture. House Lannister's gold gives the region great influence.
