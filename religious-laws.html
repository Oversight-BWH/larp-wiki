The Faith of the Seven

The Faith of the Seven is a recognized religious institution within the Realm. It maintains its own traditions, clergy, sacred places, and customary practices. The Faith does not replace the Crown’s secular authority; rather, the two jurisdictions coexist under a clear principle: the Crown governs the Realm, and the Faith governs matters of faith.

Where secular and religious authority overlap, the Law determines which jurisdiction prevails. This balance keeps the Realm stable while respecting the spiritual life of its people.

Religious Offenses

Blasphemy

Deliberate and malicious desecration or public contempt directed toward the Seven or sacred institutions may constitute blasphemy where recognized by Law.

Desecration

Destruction, defacement, theft, or profanation of a sacred object or holy site is an offense.

Heresy

Heresy may only be treated as a religious offense where recognized by lawful religious authority. It does not automatically authorize imprisonment or execution.

Attack against clergy

Violence against a Septon or Septa may be treated as an aggravated offense when motivated by their religious office.

Profanation of holy ground

Unlawful violence within a designated sacred site may receive aggravated treatment.

Shared Jurisdiction

Religious law applies to matters of faith, doctrine, ritual, and sacred practice. Civil law governs the secular order, public peace, and political institutions. Both forms of authority must operate within the larger framework of the legal system, and neither is allowed to claim total supremacy over the other.
