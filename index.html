The Code of Laws & Governance

Blood Without Honor

Welcome to the Realm

This wiki documents the foundational legal framework that governs the Realm. It establishes the institutions of power, defines the limits of authority, and ensures that all subjects—regardless of rank or station—answer to the Law.

The Three Pillars

⛩️ The Crown Governs

The reigning King or Queen holds the highest sovereign authority, subject to the laws of succession and the constraints of the Law itself. Royal authority exists to serve the Realm, not personal ambition.

⚖️ The Law Binds

No rank, house, title, wealth, or influence places an individual beyond the reach of the Law. The Law applies equally, ensuring that authority cannot become tyranny.

🗡️ Justice Preserves

A fair judiciary, proper legal procedure, and proportionate punishment preserve the stability and trust necessary for the Realm to endure.

"Power without Law becomes tyranny. Law without justice becomes oppression. The Realm requires both."

Key Principles

  • Presumption of Innocence: An accusation is not guilt. A legal process must determine responsibility.
  • Rule of Law: All authority—including the Crown—operates within established legal limits.
  • Proportionate Justice: Punishment must be appropriate to the offense and circumstances.
  • Due Process: The accused has the right to understand charges, present defense, and appeal.
  • Judicial Independence: Courts interpret and apply Law based on evidence and legal principle, not politics or personal preference.
  • No Arbitrary Authority: Officials must act within their jurisdiction and for lawful purposes only.

This code represents the foundational legal framework of the Realm. Local lords may possess authority over their territories subject to these principles, and the Crown may issue decrees that do not conflict with established Law.
