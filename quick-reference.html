At a glance

Topic Quick answer
Highest authority The Crown

What is required before execution A lawful judicial process for capital offenses

Most important offenses

Assault

Use or attempted use of unlawful force.

Theft

Unlawful taking of property.

Murder

Unlawful killing with intent or major culpability.

Treason

Betrayal of the lawful Crown or Realm.

Kidnapping

Unlawful seizure or confinement of a person.

Corruption

Misuse of office, authority, or gifts for unlawful ends.

Legal maxim

Power without Law becomes tyranny. Law without justice becomes oppression. The Realm requires both.
